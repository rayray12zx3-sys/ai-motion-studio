## Observations extracted from displayed decoded frames

**A. Photo print product demo:** warm off-white wordmark and isolated dot → pill/circle aperture → multi-photo gallery → immersive single photo and adjustment interaction → lockscreen/phone/web desktop → configurable print/product order → framed real-world physical output → return to identity. Meaningful product transformation and coherent object-lineage are more important than replicating its exact visual effect.

**B. UGC generation tool:** bold word-by-word hook → real input UI and interaction → many actual-looking vertical clip examples → sorting of generated ideas / clips → curved video wall → chosen clip and result panels → very large figures/short outcome words → concise final logo. Treat numeric claims as **content seen in a reference**, not evidence for the user's product. Images/videos/media rights require proof for any commercial reuse.

**C. Food request product:** text/type and phone interaction → enlarging/highlighting request vocabulary → abstract waiting/processing mark → result cards and selected dish → message/confirm buttons → visually reduced closing copy/brand. Fast branded UI behavior relies on causality from button/text to subsequent result; later sparse frames still intentionally allow text to land.

## What to generalize, and what not

Generalize: shot intent and action-reaction, semantic hero identity over screen handoffs, real content/compositing that shows product value, meaningful cursor gestures and responsive states, appropriate motion blur and sound cue aligning, outcome/CTA legibility, negative prompts that rule out **unmotivated** effects, deterministic `seek(t)` + actual ffprobe/movie QA.

Do not generalize: same camera route/shape sequence/card wall, 120 or 130 BPM imposed on every video, fixed transitions or banned-effects list, arbitrary 3D everywhere, 60fps assumed as universal, claims inferred from promo UI/screenshots, music/stock-photo licenses inferred from visible content, or copying third-party input/prompt into product as house template.
