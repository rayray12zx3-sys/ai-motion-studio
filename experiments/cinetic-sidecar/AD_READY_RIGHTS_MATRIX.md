## Distinguish two specific outcomes

**For this original public-fictional-only vector+PNG animation lane**, licence terms for required software and OFL font do not impose attribution **in the finished advertisement**. The PNG image format is patent-free according to W3C. The files can be incorporated into a legal commercial ad once the final visual/content and downstream publishing rules are accepted. This is a **narrow format-and-asset clearance**, not blanket certificate covering arbitrary commercial deployments.

**For an actual client's advertisement with client assets, H.264/ProRes or music**, document the client's content/brand rights, any actual service licence fees and chosen final codec/territory/distribution obligations first. Do not approve it by reusing this generic original-demo clearance. Software/font bundles distributed to others can carry separate mandatory licence/NOTICE files outside the ad.
