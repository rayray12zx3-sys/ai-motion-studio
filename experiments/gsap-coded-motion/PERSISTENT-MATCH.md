# Persistent matched foreground object across unlike 3D scenes
Source-backed motion principle: an important subject maintains identity and projected screen location through a camera/scene transition. This adds two separately rendered but identical original THREE.TorusGeometry motifs, each recomputed from its actual independently moving perspective camera at any frame. They maintain the same screen-space location, pixel radius, material and fade envelope while the authored GPU blend changes the background/hero shots. Both cameras and rig geometry remain genuinely 3D; do not mistake this controlled cinematic continuity prop for a 2D fake depth rig. Dedicated unit test verifies subpixel position matching after arbitrary out-of-order frame seeks; real browser shader/film checks remain required.
